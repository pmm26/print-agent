Scan for nearby devices, then pair each printer you want to use. You can pair more than one.

Ubuntu Bluetooth Settings can interfere with setup

GNOME searches for devices while its Bluetooth panel is open. Close that panel before a protocol-specific scan, or run:

pkill -f '^/usr/bin/gnome-control-center bluetooth$'

This closes only the GNOME settings panel; it does not stop BlueZ. On another Linux desktop, close its Bluetooth settings window manually. Do not stop bluetooth.service.
